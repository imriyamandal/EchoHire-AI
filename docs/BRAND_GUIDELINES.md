# BRAND_GUIDELINES.md — EchoHire AI Brand System

## Brand Core Identity
- **Name:** EchoHire AI
- **Tagline:** *The Interview Coach That Listens Like a Human.*
- **Mission:** Democratizing world-class technical and behavioral interview preparation through empathetic, full-duplex conversational voice intelligence.

---

## 2. Typography Hierarchy
- **Display Headings:** *Space Grotesk* (Tech-forward, bold geometry, high impact)
- **Interface & Body:** *Inter* (Crisp readability at small sizes, optimal UI clarity)
- **Telemetry & Metrics:** *JetBrains Mono / Fira Code* (Scientific precision, latency timers)

---

## 3. Motion Principles
- **Acoustic Responsiveness:** Audio waveforms pulse with real-time FFT frequency bin heights.
- **Glassmorphic Depth:** Subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-xl`), 1px semi-transparent borders (`border-slate-800/80`), and radial ambient backlights.
- **Immediate Micro-Interactions:** Active states scale down slightly (`active:scale-[0.98]`) for tactile physical weight.
